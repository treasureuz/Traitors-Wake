# Traitor's Wake
- Different paths/lanes/lines created that the user must 1:1 recreate to pass the level
- The path is highlighted after each play
- The user is only given a few seconds to memorize the highlighted path
- The paths and the grid width and height change based on difficulty

Story: A fellow pirate betrayed your shipmates by relaying confidential information to nearby rival pirate ships. With a bounty on his head, this traitor escaped onto his own ship and is headed to the mainland. Your job as the captain is to capture him by memorizing and following his trail on the sea. Beware! His routes get more difficult and trickier, and cannonballs are fired to slow you down. Do not worry! Collecting nearby chests grants power-ups. You have 5 lives to spare, but every death or mismatch resets your current stage progress. In the final stage, you must either kill him or mirror his wake to win the game.
